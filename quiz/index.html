What do you enjoy?

Ten quick questions. In each pair, pick the one that sounds more fun. There’s no right answer, and you can skip any.

Your answers only change the order of courses. They never hide a course or change whether you’re ready for it, and they stay on your device.

  1. Build a robot that can walk or Work out why some people get sick and others do not
  2. Write code for an app millions of people use or Design a bridge that survives an earthquake
  3. Find a pattern hidden in a huge pile of numbers or Grow a crop that survives a drought
  4. Design a rocket or a satellite or Invent a better battery
  5. Stop hackers breaking into a hospital’s computers or Help someone who feels anxious feel better
  6. Understand why people make the choices they do or Work out how the circuits in your phone work
  7. Make a material stronger than steel and lighter than plastic or Teach a computer to recognise voices
  8. Protect an endangered animal or Design a city that runs on wind and solar power
  9. Design aircraft and drones or Study cells and DNA in a lab
  10. Crack a secret code or Take a machine apart and make it work better
