About Path to Uni

Path to Uni is a free website that helps NSW students and parents see which university STEM courses their HSC subjects prepare them for. Pick your subjects, and it shows which courses at six universities you’re ready for, what you’d need to catch up on, and some courses you may never have heard of.

It’s not UAC or a university, and it doesn’t predict ATARs or offers. Always check UAC and the university’s website before you apply.

Who built this

I’m Ajay Walia, a Sydney parent with more than 25 years in technology. I started as an educator, teaching software engineering, and today I work on technology transition and transformation.

I built Path to Uni while helping my own child choose HSC subjects. The facts a family needs were scattered across six university websites, UAC and NESA, and most advice answered “can I get in?” when the real question was “what does this choice cost me?”. So every rule here is the university’s own wording, with a link and the date we checked it.

This is a personal, independent project — not connected with my employer, UAC or any university. I write about technology and AI at CuriousBit, and you can find me on LinkedIn.

Privacy

There’s no account, and nothing about you is stored on a server. Your subjects are kept in the part of the results link after the #, which browsers never send to a website. That’s why you can share the link (with a careers adviser, say) without us ever seeing your subjects.

The only thing the site saves on your device is whether you picked the dark or light theme. It never leaves your device, it isn’t in the shared link, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Common questions

Will this tell me my ATAR, or whether I’ll get in?

No. It shows how well your subjects prepare you for each course and what you’d need to catch up on. It doesn’t predict ATARs or offers. Your ATAR comes from UAC, and this year’s Year 12s get theirs on Wed 16 Dec 2026, 9am.

Do I need certain subjects to get into a course?

Hardly ever. Of the 6 universities here, only the University of Sydney and the University of Wollongong have a must-have subject rule, and only on 30 of the courses we list. For everything else the subjects are “assumed knowledge”. Read more about prerequisites.

What does “assumed knowledge” mean?

It’s what a course expects you to know when you start. It doesn’t stop you applying, but if you haven’t studied it you’ll need to catch up, and some universities run short bridging courses for that. Read more about assumed knowledge.

Do Year 11 results matter?

Yes, for early offers. Macquarie, Western Sydney and Wollongong can make you an offer before your ATAR comes out, based partly on your Year 11 results. So can UNSW’s Gateway scheme, for students who meet certain equity criteria. All the dates are on Key dates.

Where does your information come from?

Straight from each university’s website and UAC. We quote their exact words, link to the page, and note the date we checked. We last re-checked the course rules on 24 October 2026 and the dates on 5 November 2026.

Do you store my details?

No. There’s no sign-up, and your subjects stay in your browser. They’re never sent to us. Read more about privacy.

Which universities and courses are included?

130 STEM courses, including health, at Macquarie University, UNSW, University of Sydney, University of Technology Sydney, University of Wollongong and Western Sydney University.

Is this an official site?

No. We’re independent. We’re not UAC or a university, and no one pays to be listed. Always double-check on UAC and the university’s website before you apply.
