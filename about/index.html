About Path to Uni

Path to Uni is a free website that helps NSW students and parents see which university STEM courses their HSC subjects prepare them for. Pick your subjects, and it shows which courses at six universities you’re ready for, what you’d need to catch up on, and some courses you may never have heard of.

It’s not UAC or a university, and it doesn’t predict ATARs or offers. Always check UAC and the university’s website before you apply.

Who built this

I’m Ajay Walia, a Sydney parent with more than 25 years in technology. I started as an educator, teaching software engineering, and today I work on technology transition and transformation.

I built Path to Uni while helping my own child choose HSC subjects. The facts a family needs were scattered across six university websites, UAC and NESA, and most advice answered “can I get in?” when the real question was “what does this choice cost me?”. So every rule here is the university’s own wording, with a link and the date we checked it.

This is a personal, independent project — not connected with my employer, UAC or any university. I write about technology and AI at CuriousBit, and you can find me on LinkedIn.

Privacy

There’s no account, and nothing about you is stored on a server. Your subjects are kept in the part of the results link after the #, which browsers never send to a website. That’s why you can share the link (with a careers adviser, say) without us ever seeing your subjects.

The only thing the site saves on your device is whether you picked the dark or light theme. It never leaves your device, it isn’t in the shared link, and clearing your browser data removes it.
