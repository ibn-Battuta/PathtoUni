# PathtoUni

A NSW STEM readiness and discovery planner. Enter your HSC subjects; see which degrees across six
universities you are genuinely prepared for, what any gap actually costs, and which ones you had
never heard of.

**Readiness prunes, then discovery reveals.** Roughly two hundred STEM degrees is unbrowsable;
the forty you are prepared for is a list a family can read on a Sunday afternoon.

Not UAC and not a university. Eligibility is based on published rules at the date shown.
Confirm every preference on UAC and the university handbook before you apply.
