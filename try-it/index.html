Try it before you choose

Spend a week in a lab, a day at an engineering firm, or do a first-year uni subject from home. Trying a field is the best way to find out if you’d enjoy studying it for years. Every program here says on its own website that school students can take part.

  • These aren’t internships. Real internships for under-18s are rare in Australia. What you’ll find here is work experience, one-day events, live-in programs, uni subjects, competitions, free online courses and paid school-based traineeships.
  • Dates change every year. Each one shows when we checked it. Many open months ahead and fill up early, so check the program’s website.
  • Not listed doesn’t mean no. A lab or company without its own program may still take a student. Ask your school careers adviser to set it up as work experience, so you’re safe and insured.
  • Year 12 gets busy. The summer holidays before Year 12, and Term 1, are the easiest times.
