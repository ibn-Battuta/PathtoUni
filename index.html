Common questions

Does this predict my ATAR, or my chance of an offer?

No. It shows how ready your subjects make you for each degree, and what a gap would cost — never whether you will get in. ATARs come from UAC (for this year’s Year 12: Wed 16 Dec 2026, 9am).

Do NSW degrees have subject prerequisites?

Almost never as a gate. Of the 6 universities here, only University of Sydney and University of Wollongong have a hard subject rule on the degrees we list (30 degrees). Everything else is assumed knowledge. Read the details.

What is assumed knowledge?

What a course expects you to know on day one. It does not stop you applying. It tells you what first year will feel like if you have not done the subject — sometimes a bridging course closes the gap. More.

Do Year 11 results matter?

For early offers, yes. Macquarie, Western Sydney and Wollongong make early offers that read Year 11 results, before the ATAR, and so does UNSW’s Gateway scheme for students who meet an equity criterion. Every round, with its dates, is on Key dates.

Where do the facts come from?

From each university’s own published pages and UAC, quoted word for word with the date we read them. Course rules were last re-checked on 24 October 2026; dates and programs on 5 November 2026.

Is my information stored?

No. There is no account. Your subjects stay in your browser’s address bar and are never sent to a server. More on privacy.

Which universities and degrees are covered?

130 STEM degrees, including health, at Macquarie University, UNSW, University of Sydney, University of Technology Sydney, University of Wollongong and Western Sydney University.

Is this official?
