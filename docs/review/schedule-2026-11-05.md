## What was wrong on our side

- **Error 25** — Informatics Olympiad cost shown as $25; the page says $25.90. Our quote
  "COST AUD $25." was a prefix of the real text, so it matched. Fixed; both validators now

## A tooling lesson

A text-only page reader (used for the first search pass) returned **stale copies** of
Macquarie's and QUT's pages — 2024 and 2025 dates, "applications now open". The desktop
browser showed the current pages ("Applications for 2027 entry are now closed"; QUT January 2027,
closed 31 October 2026). Nothing on the site rests on the page reader. Decision 32.

## What the 2028 view does with unpublished dates

When checked, no university had published its 2027 rounds (for 2028 entry). Events marked
`repeats` appear in the 2028 view as **"Last year's date"**, placed in the same month a year on
("around July 2027") with the 2026 dates shown as 2026 dates. They are never written as a
2027 day. Replace them from April 2027 as each university publishes.
