  results as well as unconditional ones on Year 12 results. Final 2027 round: 8 January 2027.

  own 2026 FAQ article.

## What the site had wrong all along (found because the capture was read again)

- **Error 22** — Wollongong Mathematics and Engineering (Hons)/Mathematics have a published
  prerequisite; the site had none.
- **Error 23** — Wollongong Computer Science: "Mathematics Advanced or Mathematics Standard 2" was
  stored as both, giving Standard 2 students a false gap.
- **Error 24** — omissions: Wollongong Nursing, Mathematics and IT showed no assumed knowledge;
  Wollongong recommended studies were empty; Macquarie's "Band 4" was dropped; UTS and Wollongong
  English lines were missing.

## How to repeat it

`/tmp`-style scripts are not kept; the method is: fetch each page from its own origin in the
browser, run `pipeline/render.js` `htmlToText` and the `section()` reader from
`pipeline/html-course.js` on it, and compare with the same reader run on `captures/` on disk.
Then run `extractCoursePage` on every capture and compare its subjects with `data/courses/` —
that second step is what found errors 22–24.
