# NSW admission rules — verified 27 August 2026

Re-verify before encoding anything. Selection ranks and assumed knowledge both move.

## Hard subject prerequisites barely exist

| University | Hard prerequisites | Verbatim |
|---|---|---|
| UNSW | None | "we don't have formal subject prerequisites for any of our degrees, we have assumed knowledge" |
| UTS | None except teaching | "There are no HSC subject prerequisites for any UTS course, with the exception of teacher education courses." |
| Sydney | Engineering (Hons), Advanced Computing, Pharmacy | "Mathematics Advanced (Band 4) or Mathematics Extension 1 or 2 (Band E3)" |
| Macquarie | None | "it's not a requirement for acceptance into a degree" |
| Western Sydney | None | "you are still eligible to apply for that course" |
| Wollongong | None | "Assumed knowledge is not a compulsory requirement for admission" |

Sydney dropped maths prerequisites for Commerce, Science, Medicine, Psychology, Veterinary Science
and Economics from the 2025 intake — an access and equity decision, explicitly not a claim that
maths stopped mattering.

## But subject background genuinely predicts success

ACDS / ACER (McMillan & Edwards), 16,436 first-year students, 12 Australian universities: higher
Year 12 maths predicts passing first-year maths and science subjects, and the effect **remains
statistically significant after controlling for ATAR**. Subject background carries independent
predictive power.

This is why the product exists. Universities are removing formal requirements while first-year
difficulty is unchanged, so the gap between "what is required" and "what you need to cope" is widening.

**Evidence asymmetry:** that research is about *mathematics*. Physics-to-engineering and
chemistry-to-health are much thinner. Never render them with equal confidence.

## NESA rules that bite

- **Extension 2 unit recount** — "For students entered in Mathematics Extension 2, both Mathematics
  Extension 1 and Mathematics Extension 2 are counted as 2-unit courses." Extension 1 is 1 unit only
  when studied with Mathematics Advanced. So an Extension 2 student carries 4 units of maths and
  **does not sit Mathematics Advanced** — which removes the easiest route through Sydney's gate.
- **Subject areas are not courses** — "Within an HSC subject area (eg mathematics) there may be a
  number of courses." All maths courses collapse to one subject area; Physics and Chemistry are two.
- **ATAR eligibility** — at least 10 units of Board Developed courses, 2 units of English, three
  courses of 2 units or more, four subject areas. ATAR = best 2 units English + best 8 from the rest.
- **Retired courses** — Software Design and Development was replaced by Software Engineering from
  2024 (first HSC 2025). Information Processes and Technology gave way to Enterprise Computing.
- **Live syllabus change** — the Mathematics Extension 1 11–12 Syllabus (2024) began Year 11 in
  Term 1 2026, first HSC examination 2027. University assumed-knowledge statements were written
  against the old syllabus.

## Admission is not only prerequisites

Three of the six lead with non-ATAR routes that read Year 11 results — see `data/early-entry.json`.
Western Sydney's HSC True Reward admits on **HSC subject bands rather than the ATAR**, which makes
subject choice feed admission directly. A tool modelling only prerequisites describes a minority
of how NSW admission actually works.
