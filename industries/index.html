Where is the world investing?

These areas are named in official plans by Australia, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, China and India. Every quote below is the government’s own words, with a link and the date we checked it.

  • A signal, not a promise. Government money shows where jobs may grow. It does not guarantee a job, and plans change after elections.
  • Missing is not “no”. If a country is not listed for an area, we did not find it in the documents we read. It may still be investing.
  • Think in decades. A degree that starts in 2028 finishes around 2031 or later. Broad skills — maths, coding, lab work — travel between these areas.
