Jobs of the future

These fields appear in official investment plans from Australia, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, China, India, Japan and South Korea. Each quote below is the government’s own words, with a link and the date we checked it.

  • A signal, not a promise. Government money shows where jobs may grow. It doesn’t guarantee a job, and plans can change after an election.
  • Missing doesn’t mean no. If a country isn’t listed for a field, we didn’t find it in the documents we read. It may still be investing.
  • Think long term. A degree that starts in 2028 finishes around 2031 or later. Skills like maths, coding and lab work carry across all of these fields.
