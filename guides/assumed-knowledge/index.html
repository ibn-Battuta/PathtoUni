Assumed knowledge vs prerequisites: what’s the difference?

Universities use three terms that sound alike but mean very different things.

Prerequisite (rare in NSW): a result you must reach before you can get an offer. Sydney’s maths mark for a handful of courses is one. So is Wollongong’s Mathematics Standard 2 rule for its two maths degrees.

Assumed knowledge: the university teaches as if you’ve already done that HSC subject. You can still get in, but first year may be harder, and there may be a bridging course to help you catch up.

Recommended studies: just a suggestion. It’s not assumed knowledge or a prerequisite, and we never count it as a gap.

It’s easy to mix up assumed knowledge and recommended studies — we did once. Our course pages now keep them clearly apart.
