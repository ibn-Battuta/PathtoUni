What happens if you drop a subject?

For a typical STEM mix, dropping History changes nothing here, because engineering and science courses don’t assume it. Dropping Physics often does change things.

There’s a second thing to check, which has nothing to do with universities: the rules for getting an ATAR. You need at least 10 units of Board Developed courses (the standard HSC courses), 2 units of English, three courses of 2 units or more, and subjects from four subject areas.

If you take Extension 2, your Extension 1 counts as 2 units. So with Extension 2, dropping both History and Engineering Studies can still leave you with exactly 10 units and four areas. Without it, the same move can drop you to 9 units, and you wouldn’t get an ATAR.

On your results page, What if you dropped a subject? checks every subject you’re taking, one at a time, and tells you either nothing changes or how many courses get harder.
