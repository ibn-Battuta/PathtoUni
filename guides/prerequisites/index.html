Do you need physics for engineering in NSW?

Almost never as a must-have. Of the six universities here, only two have a strict subject rule on the courses we list:

  • The University of Sydney needs a certain mark in maths for Engineering (Honours), Advanced Computing and Pharmacy.
  • Wollongong’s Bachelor of Mathematics, and its Engineering (Honours)/Mathematics double degree, need you to have studied Mathematics Standard 2 or equivalent, at Band 2 or higher.

Everything else is assumed knowledge.

Most NSW universities publish assumed knowledge. It doesn’t stop you applying. It tells you what first year will be like if you haven’t done the subject.

From the 2025 intake, Sydney dropped its maths prerequisites for Commerce, Science, Medicine, Psychology, Veterinary Science and Economics. That made those courses easier to get into. It doesn’t mean maths stopped mattering.

So when you’re choosing HSC subjects, don’t just ask whether a door is locked. Ask what a gap will cost you: a bridging course, or a harder first year.
