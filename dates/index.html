Key dates

Early offers, UAC offer rounds, open days, holiday programs and work experience, in date order. Choose the year you start university.

  • Every date comes from the page that set it. Each one is quoted word for word from the university’s, UAC’s or the program’s own page — open “What their own page says”. Checked 5 November 2026.
  • “Last year’s date” means not published yet. No university had announced its 2027 rounds when we checked. We show the 2026 date so you know roughly when to look, never a guessed day.
  • “For some students only” marks schemes for students who meet an equity criterion, such as living in a low-socioeconomic area or being Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander.
  • An early offer is a separate process from the subjects you choose. Most read Year 11 results; none of them is a score or a likelihood, and we do not predict offers.

What to do in Year 11

  1. Year 11 results count. Macquarie, Western Sydney and Wollongong read them for early offers, and so does UNSW Gateway. See: Macquarie, Western Sydney, Wollongong, UNSW
  2. Keep a list of what you do outside class in Years 11 and 12 — sport, volunteering, part-time work, leadership. Macquarie asks for it. See: Macquarie
  3. Use the summer holidays before Year 12. They are the easiest time to try a field. See: UNSW
  4. Go to open days in Year 11 (last year they ran from late June to early September), not in Year 12. See: Wollongong, UNSW
  5. Ask your careers adviser now about school work experience and programs only schools can nominate for.
  6. Early-offer applications for 2028 entry should open around April–June 2027. Check each university's page then — the dates below are last year's. See: Western Sydney, UNSW, Wollongong

Any time — work experience and internships

Real internships for under-18s are rare in Australia. These have no fixed date: ask your school careers adviser, who can arrange work experience at almost any lab or company and keeps it safe and insured.

  • School work experience — Your school (NSW Department of Education). Ask your school careers adviser. The school arranges it, which keeps it safe and insured.
  • Computer Science High School Fellowship (INFO1110) — University of Sydney, School of Computer Science. Only through your school careers adviser.
  • School-based apprenticeship or traineeship — NSW Department of Education. Ask your school. It changes your HSC pattern, so ask how it affects your ATAR first.
  • AWS Educate — Amazon Web Services. Register online yourself.
  • Work experience at Mount Stromlo Observatory — Australian National University. Apply through the ANU page.

Not on this list

UTS has stopped its Early Entry Program from 2027 entry. UTS still takes part in the Schools Recommendation Scheme, but only for students eligible for certain Educational Access Schemes.
