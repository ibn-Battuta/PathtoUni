Key dates

Every date that matters on the way to uni, from early offers and open days to holiday programs, in one list. Choose the year you’ll start university.

How to read these dates

Where does each date come from?

From the website that set it — the university, UAC or the program itself — quoted word for word. Open “What their website says” under any date to see the quote and the link. Checked 5 November 2026.

What does “Last year’s date” mean?

The new date isn’t out yet. No university had announced its 2027 rounds when we checked, so we show the 2026 date to give you a rough idea. We never guess a day.

What does “For some students only” mean?

The scheme is for students who meet certain equity criteria, such as living in a lower-income area or being Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander.

Does an early offer depend on my subjects?

No, it’s separate from the subjects you choose. Most early offers look at your Year 11 results. We don’t predict who gets an offer.

What to do in Year 11

  1. Year 11 results count. Macquarie, Western Sydney and Wollongong read them for early offers, and so does UNSW Gateway. See: Macquarie, Western Sydney, Wollongong, UNSW
  2. Keep a list of what you do outside class in Years 11 and 12 — sport, volunteering, part-time work, leadership. Macquarie asks for it. See: Macquarie
  3. Use the summer holidays before Year 12. They are the easiest time to try a field. See: UNSW
  4. Go to open days in Year 11 (last year they ran from late June to early September), not in Year 12. See: Wollongong, UNSW
  5. Ask your careers adviser now about school work experience and programs only schools can nominate for.
  6. Early-offer applications for 2028 entry should open around April–June 2027. Check each university's page then — the dates below are last year's. See: Western Sydney, UNSW, Wollongong

Any time — work experience and internships

Real internships for under-18s are rare in Australia. These have no set date. Your school careers adviser can arrange work experience at almost any lab or company, and makes sure you’re safe and insured.

  • School work experience — Your school (NSW Department of Education). Ask your school careers adviser. The school arranges it, which keeps it safe and insured.
  • Computer Science High School Fellowship (INFO1110) — University of Sydney, School of Computer Science. Only through your school careers adviser.
  • GitHub Student Developer Pack — GitHub. Apply online yourself with proof that you are a student, such as a school ID.
  • School-based apprenticeship or traineeship — NSW Department of Education. Ask your school. It changes your HSC pattern, so ask how it affects your ATAR first.
  • AWS Educate — Amazon Web Services. Register online yourself.
  • Work experience at Mount Stromlo Observatory — Australian National University. Apply through the ANU page.

After Year 12 — paid work, apprenticeships and cadetships

These pay you, and they’re for school leavers, not current students. Some fit around a uni degree; others are a job with training instead of full-time uni. Paid tech internships mostly start once you’re at uni.
